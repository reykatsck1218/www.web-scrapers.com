Web scraping powers everything from price intelligence to market research — but every industry and target site brings its own challenges. These solution guides pair practical strategy with **ready-to-run code samples in PHP, Node.js, and Rust**, so you can go from idea to working scraper fast.

Start with our [E-commerce overview](/solutions/ecommerce/), then jump into a hands-on tracker for [Amazon](/solutions/amazon-product-tracking/), [Walmart](/solutions/walmart-product-tracking/), [AliExpress](/solutions/aliexpress-product-tracking/), or [Alibaba](/solutions/alibaba-scraping/) — or scrape search results with our [Google Search](/solutions/google-search-scraping/) guide.

Working with travel or social data? We cover [Airbnb](/solutions/airbnb-scraping/), [Booking.com](/solutions/booking-scraping/), and [Kayak flight prices](/solutions/kayak-flight-scraping/) on the travel side, plus public data from [TikTok](/solutions/tiktok-scraping/), [Instagram](/solutions/instagram-scraping/), [Facebook](/solutions/facebook-scraping/), and [LinkedIn](/solutions/linkedin-scraping/).
