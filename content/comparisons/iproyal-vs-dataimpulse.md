## Proxy Networks and Types

### IPRoyal's network

IPRoyal's Royal Residential pool consists of **millions of ethically sourced residential IPs** with targeting down to country, state, and city level. Around that core it offers one of the broadest lineups in the budget segment: static residential (ISP) proxies for stable, trusted sessions; datacenter proxies for cheap, fast volume; mobile proxies for high-trust carrier IPs; and dedicated **sneaker proxies** — a niche product few budget competitors bother with. Sessions can be rotating or sticky, and **SOCKS5 support** gives you protocol flexibility beyond plain HTTP.

### DataImpulse's network

DataImpulse also runs **millions of ethically sourced residential IPs, spread across virtually every country**, alongside mobile, datacenter, and ISP proxies — all managed from a single dashboard. Targeting covers country, region, and city, with sticky or rotating sessions. The integration story is deliberately plain: a standard endpoint and credentials that work with any scraper or HTTP client in any language. Where IPRoyal differentiates on lineup breadth, DataImpulse differentiates on keeping everything simple and in one place.

### Who wins on the network?

Call it a narrow win for IPRoyal on versatility — sneaker proxies and SOCKS5 are real, if niche, advantages, and state-level targeting is a touch more specific than region-level. But for the core use cases both providers are chasing — rotating residential and mobile scraping on a budget — the networks are functionally comparable. ## Standout Features

### IPRoyal: traffic that never expires

IPRoyal's headline perk is **non-expiring residential traffic**. The GBs you buy sit in your account indefinitely — no monthly reset, no quiet expiry. For occasional or seasonal scrapers this is genuinely valuable: you can buy a chunk of traffic during a promotion, run your quarterly price-monitoring job, and still have the balance waiting six months later. It converts proxy bandwidth from a subscription-shaped cost into something closer to a stocked pantry.

### DataImpulse: rock-bottom pricing, zero friction

DataImpulse is built around **being one of the cheapest residential options on the market** and making the buying process trivially simple. You top up a balance, pay per GB against it, and never touch a subscription. There is no clever perk to model — the pitch is simply that the same everyday scraping costs less here, and the unified dashboard means adding mobile, datacenter, or ISP proxies later doesn't mean learning a new product.

## Pricing

Both providers use **affordable pay-as-you-go pricing with no forced monthly commitment** — that shared philosophy is why they end up head-to-head on so many shortlists. The differences are at the margins:

- **IPRoyal** is competitively priced per GB, with the standout benefit that purchased residential traffic doesn't expire. Its effective cost advantage shows up over time: irregular users waste nothing.
- **DataImpulse** is among the most affordable residential proxy providers, full stop. The top-up balance model makes it trivial to start small — fund the balance, scrape, refill when needed — and easy to scale spend up or down as projects come and go.

The practical guidance: if you scrape steadily and price-per-GB is your only metric, DataImpulse's positioning is hard to beat. If you scrape in bursts with quiet months in between, IPRoyal's non-expiring traffic may save you more than a lower sticker price would.
