If you've priced out residential proxies from the big enterprise providers and winced, IPRoyal is one of the first names you should look at next. It's a well-rounded, budget-to-mid-market proxy provider that has earned a loyal following for flexible, affordable plans — and one standout perk you won't find at most competitors: residential traffic that never expires. Buy GBs today, use them next quarter. For anyone whose scraping is irregular, that single feature changes the math.

I've used IPRoyal as a "second provider" alongside premium networks for a while now, and that's exactly the role it plays best: dependable, ethically sourced proxies for everyday targets, at a price that doesn't punish you for scraping in bursts. Here's the full picture — what you get, what it costs in structural terms, and where it genuinely can't keep up with the top tier.

Who Is IPRoyal?

IPRoyal sits in the crowded middle of the proxy market, below enterprise platforms like Bright Data and Oxylabs and above the fly-by-night resellers you should avoid entirely. Its positioning is clear and consistent: ethically sourced IPs, a full range of proxy types, and pay-as-you-go billing with no forced monthly subscription.

That last part matters more than it sounds. Most residential proxy providers sell you a monthly allocation that resets whether you used it or not — which quietly turns "cheap per GB" into "expensive per GB actually used" for anyone who doesn't scrape on a steady schedule. IPRoyal's model is the opposite: you buy traffic, it sits in your account, and it's there whenever your next project kicks off.

Product Lineup

IPRoyal covers every major proxy type from one dashboard, which makes it a genuine one-stop shop for general-purpose work. (If you're not sure which type you need, our proxy types guide breaks down the trade-offs.)

Royal Residential Proxies

The flagship product: millions of ethically sourced residential IPs with country, state, and city-level targeting. This is where the non-expiring traffic perk applies, and it's the product most scrapers will buy. Sessions can be rotating — a fresh IP per request or interval, ideal for spreading load across the pool — or sticky, holding one IP for tasks that need a consistent identity.

Static Residential (ISP) Proxies

ISP proxies are the hybrid option: hosted on datacenter infrastructure for speed, but registered under real ISPs for residential-level trust. IPRoyal's static residential line is a good fit for account management, sustained logged-in sessions, and retail tasks where you need the same trusted IP for hours at a time.

Datacenter Proxies

The cheap, fast workhorses. Best for high-volume scraping of targets with weak or no anti-bot protection, where paying residential rates would be wasteful.

Mobile Proxies

Real mobile-carrier IPs for the targets that block everything else — social platforms and mobile-first apps chiefly. The hardest IP type to ban, and priced accordingly across the industry.

Sneaker Proxies

A niche IPRoyal serves that most budget competitors don't: proxies tuned for sneaker and retail-drop sites. If this is your use case, it's one of the few affordable providers that treats it as a first-class product rather than an afterthought.

Key Features

  • Non-expiring residential traffic. The headline. GBs you buy don't expire, so nothing you pay for goes to waste — genuinely rare and genuinely useful.
  • Ethically sourced pool. Millions of residential IPs sourced with consent, which matters for both reliability and staying on the right side of your own compliance bar.
  • Granular geo-targeting. Country, state, and city-level targeting on residential — finer than many budget rivals offer.
  • Flexible sessions. Rotating or sticky, with SOCKS5 support alongside standard HTTP(S), so it slots into any scraper, HTTP client, or automation tool without adapters.
  • Every major proxy type. Residential, ISP, datacenter, mobile, and sneaker proxies from one account.
  • 24/7 support. Live support on every plan, not just enterprise tiers.

Pricing: Pay As You Go, Nothing Wasted

IPRoyal uses an affordable pay-as-you-go model, with residential proxies among the more competitively priced in the market. There's no mandatory subscription and no monthly minimum: you top up, you scrape, and whatever you don't use waits for you.

Two structural points worth weighing:

  1. The non-expiring traffic effectively lowers your real cost. With subscription providers, unused allocation is money burned. With IPRoyal, your effective per-GB price is the sticker price, full stop.
  2. Per-GB billing favors light pages. Like most proxy providers, you're billed on bandwidth. Scraping lean HTML or JSON endpoints stretches your GBs a long way; heavy media-laden pages burn them faster. (This is the same billing-model trade-off we cover in our ZenRows alternatives guide.)

Performance: What to Expect

Let's be honest about the tier IPRoyal plays in. On mainstream targets — the vast majority of e-commerce, travel, real estate, and content sites — its residential and ISP networks deliver reliable success rates and dependable speeds. Rotation works as advertised, sticky sessions hold, and the geo-targeting is accurate in my experience.

Where it won't match the premium tier is on the most heavily defended sites. In our testing, Bright Data's residential network hits 99.5%+ success rates on hardened targets like Amazon, Walmart, and Google — and it pairs that network with unblocking tools (CAPTCHA solving, browser fingerprinting, automatic retries) that IPRoyal simply doesn't offer. IPRoyal gives you clean proxies; the unblocking logic is your job. If your targets sit behind aggressive anti-bot systems, budget for the premium option or a managed unblocker. For everything else, IPRoyal's value is excellent — see the full head-to-head in Bright Data vs IPRoyal.

Who It's For — and Who Should Skip It

Get IPRoyal if:

  • Your scraping is occasional, seasonal, or bursty — the non-expiring traffic is built for exactly this pattern
  • You want one affordable account covering residential, ISP, datacenter, mobile, and sneaker proxies
  • You run your own scraper code and just need clean, well-targeted IPs
  • You're leaving a subscription provider because you kept paying for GBs you never used

Skip it if:

  • Your targets are fortress-grade anti-bot deployments — go premium with Bright Data or Oxylabs
  • You want a managed scraping API that handles rendering, retries, and CAPTCHAs for you
  • You need massive concurrent scale, where the largest networks pull ahead

If you're weighing it against its budget-tier peers instead: HydraProxy undercuts it on minimum spend and adds a mobile focus, while DataImpulse competes hard on raw per-GB cost. IPRoyal beats both on breadth of proxy types and geo-targeting depth.

Conclusion

IPRoyal hits a sweet spot of price, flexibility, and features that few providers match. The non-expiring traffic alone makes it worth a look for anyone with irregular scraping needs, the proxy lineup covers essentially every everyday use case, and the pay-as-you-go model means you never pay for capacity you don't use. It isn't trying to be an enterprise unblocking platform — and priced like this, it doesn't need to be.

FAQ

Does IPRoyal residential traffic really never expire?

Yes — this is IPRoyal's signature perk. Residential GBs you purchase stay in your account until you use them, with no monthly reset and no expiry window. If you scrape quarterly, seasonally, or in unpredictable bursts, you can stock up when it's convenient and draw down on your own schedule.

Is IPRoyal good for web scraping?

For mainstream targets, absolutely. The ethically sourced residential network, granular geo-targeting, and rotating/sticky session options handle the majority of real-world scraping jobs reliably. It's a proxy service rather than an unblocking platform, though — for the most aggressively defended sites, a premium provider like Bright Data retains the edge.

How does IPRoyal compare to Bright Data?

They serve different tiers. Bright Data brings the industry's largest network — 400M+ residential IPs — plus a full unblocking toolkit, at premium prices. IPRoyal offers cheaper, no-commitment proxies with non-expiring traffic, best suited to general targets. Read the full breakdown in Bright Data vs IPRoyal.

Does IPRoyal support SOCKS5 and sticky sessions?

Yes on both counts. SOCKS5 is supported alongside standard HTTP(S), and sessions can be rotating (fresh IP per request or interval) or sticky (hold one IP for logins, checkouts, and other identity-sensitive flows).
