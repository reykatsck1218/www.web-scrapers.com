HydraProxy vs IPRoyal: Best Flexible, Low-Budget Proxies?

Compare HydraProxy and IPRoyal on proxy types, pricing, and flexibility to find the best low-budget residential and mobile proxies for scraping.

Updated 14 August 2026 9 min read

HydraProxy and IPRoyal compete for the same customer: the developer, marketer, or side-project builder who wants dependable residential proxies without signing up for a big monthly plan. Both are pay-as-you-go, both sell traffic that never expires, and both are popular with solo developers and social media managers who would rather top up a few dollars at a time than commit to an enterprise contract. The overlap is real — but so are the differences. HydraProxy is the leaner, mobile-friendly, micro-budget option; IPRoyal is the broader all-rounder with more proxy types and finer geo-targeting. This comparison walks through the networks, features, pricing models, and performance of each so you can pick the right one the first time.

Proxy Networks and Types

The clearest difference between these two providers is breadth of lineup.

HydraProxy's network

HydraProxy keeps its catalog deliberately small: rotating residential proxies plus real 4G/5G mobile IPs. The residential pool handles general scraping with automatic rotation to keep you unblocked, while the mobile proxies are the headline act. Mobile IPs come from real carrier networks, and because carriers share a small number of IPs among many legitimate users, platforms are extremely reluctant to ban them. That makes HydraProxy's mobile option genuinely useful for social media management, app-based tasks, and other account-centric work where a flagged IP is expensive. Targeting covers country and city level, with sticky or rotating sessions, and the standard proxy credentials work with any scraper or HTTP client.

IPRoyal's network

IPRoyal fields one of the widest lineups in the budget segment: residential, static residential (ISP), datacenter, mobile, and sneaker proxies, all from one account. Its Royal Residential pool consists of millions of ethically sourced IPs with country, state, and city-level targeting — a notch more granular than HydraProxy's. You also get flexible session control (rotating or sticky) and SOCKS5 support, which matters if your tooling goes beyond plain HTTP. The dedicated sneaker proxies are a niche IPRoyal serves that HydraProxy simply doesn't.

Who wins on the network?

IPRoyal, on breadth. Five proxy types versus two, plus state-level targeting and SOCKS5, make it the more versatile toolkit — if your needs might grow beyond residential scraping, IPRoyal gives you room. HydraProxy answers with focus: if what you actually need is mobile IPs for social tasks on a shoestring budget, its narrow catalog is not a limitation at all. Standout Features

Non-expiring traffic — a shared superpower

Both providers offer something most of the industry doesn't: traffic that never expires. IPRoyal's purchased residential GBs sit in your account until you use them; HydraProxy's purchased traffic and proxies likewise don't expire. For anyone who scrapes occasionally — a seasonal price-monitoring project, an ad-hoc research task, a side project that runs in bursts — this is the single most money-saving feature either provider offers. You buy once and draw down over months, instead of watching an unused monthly allowance evaporate.

Where HydraProxy stands out

  • Very low minimum top-ups. HydraProxy is built for micro-budgets. You can start with a tiny top-up, which makes it ideal for testing a scraper, running a one-off job, or dipping a toe into proxies for the first time.
  • Mobile-first flexibility. Real 4G/5G IPs give you the highest-trust IP type available for social platforms and mobile-only content.
  • Simplicity. A small product surface means less to learn — credentials in, traffic out.

Where IPRoyal stands out

  • Proxy-type coverage. Residential, ISP, datacenter, mobile, and sneaker proxies under one roof mean you rarely need a second provider as requirements shift.
  • Finer geo-targeting. Country, state, and city targeting supports localized SERP checks and region-specific price monitoring.
  • SOCKS5 support and 24/7 support. Useful protocol flexibility, plus live support available on every plan.

Pricing

Neither provider publishes enterprise-style contracts — both are low-cost and pay-as-you-go with no monthly commitment, which is exactly why they end up on the same shortlist.

  • HydraProxy bills residential and mobile usage as you go, and its very small minimum top-ups make it the cheaper provider to start with. If your entire budget for an experiment is pocket change, HydraProxy is the rare provider that accommodates that.
  • IPRoyal is competitively priced per GB, and the non-expiring residential traffic changes the effective economics: you can buy when prices or promotions suit you and spend the balance whenever the work actually arrives.

The honest comparison is that both are budget options and the difference at typical small-project volumes is modest. What tilts the decision is shape of usage: tiny, immediate needs favor HydraProxy's low entry point; irregular usage over a long horizon favors IPRoyal's buy-and-bank model.

Performance

Both providers deliver reliable results on mainstream targets — standard e-commerce pages, content sites, search results, and the broad middle of the web. HydraProxy is particularly handy for mobile-IP use cases like managing social accounts, where its carrier IPs earn a level of trust that residential pools can't match. IPRoyal is the dependable all-rounder, with solid success rates across its residential and ISP networks.

Where both hit their ceiling is the hardest anti-bot systems. Neither offers the advanced unblocking machinery — automated CAPTCHA solving, fingerprint management, dedicated unlocker products — that enterprise platforms bring. If your targets are aggressively defended, our guide on how to avoid getting blocked covers the tactics, and a premium provider like Bright Data remains the stronger tool for that fight.

Ease of Use

Both are refreshingly simple compared with enterprise platforms. HydraProxy's small catalog means the dashboard is quick to learn: pick a proxy product, top up, grab credentials. IPRoyal's broader lineup adds a little more surface area, but each proxy type works with standard credentials and the usual rotation/sticky options, so integration into any scraper or HTTP client is straightforward on both. Neither requires proxy expertise to get productive on day one.

When to Choose HydraProxy

HydraProxy is the better fit if:

  • Your budget is tiny. The very low minimum top-ups make it the cheapest way to get real residential and mobile proxies into a project.
  • You need mobile IPs. Real 4G/5G carrier IPs are the strongest option for social media management, account-based tasks, and mobile-first platforms.
  • You want minimal complexity. Two focused products, simple billing, nothing to configure beyond credentials.
  • You're testing. Low entry cost plus non-expiring traffic means a failed experiment wastes almost nothing.

When to Choose IPRoyal

IPRoyal is the better fit if:

  • You want one provider for many jobs. Residential, ISP, datacenter, mobile, and sneaker proxies cover nearly every everyday use case.
  • You scrape occasionally or seasonally. Non-expiring residential traffic means GBs bought today are still there next quarter.
  • You need finer geo-targeting. Country, state, and city-level targeting supports localized scraping that country-only targeting can't.
  • Your tooling needs SOCKS5. Protocol flexibility that not every budget provider offers.
  • You want a dependable all-rounder. The broader network and feature set earn it the higher rating of the two in our reviews.

Verdict: HydraProxy vs IPRoyal

We rate IPRoyal 4.2/5 and HydraProxy 3.9/5, and the gap mirrors the breadth difference: IPRoyal simply does more — more proxy types, finer targeting, SOCKS5 — while matching HydraProxy's flexible, non-expiring, pay-as-you-go model. For most developers who want a single budget proxy provider they won't outgrow quickly, IPRoyal is the default recommendation.

But HydraProxy keeps a real lane. If your project is small, social, or mobile-centric — or your budget is measured in single top-ups — its ultra-low entry point and 4G/5G mobile IPs make it the sharper tool for the job. Because both are pay-as-you-go with non-expiring traffic, trying each against your actual targets costs very little; let success rates on your own domains make the final call.

FAQ

Is HydraProxy cheaper than IPRoyal?

Both are budget, pay-as-you-go providers, so neither carries enterprise-level bills. HydraProxy's distinguishing trait is its very low minimum top-up, which makes the absolute cost of getting started lower. IPRoyal is competitively priced per GB, and its non-expiring residential traffic lets you buy ahead without waste. Promotional rates change often, so check current pricing on both sites before committing.

Does traffic expire on HydraProxy or IPRoyal?

No — this is a perk both providers share. Traffic purchased from HydraProxy doesn't expire, and IPRoyal's residential GBs never expire either. That makes both well suited to occasional or seasonal scraping, where a use-it-or-lose-it monthly allowance would waste money.

Which is better for social media tasks, HydraProxy or IPRoyal?

HydraProxy, in most cases. Its real 4G/5G mobile proxies use carrier-assigned IPs that platforms are very reluctant to ban, since each IP is shared by many legitimate users. IPRoyal also offers mobile proxies, but HydraProxy's lineup is more focused on social and app-based work.

Can HydraProxy or IPRoyal handle heavily protected sites?

Both perform well on mainstream targets, but neither is built for the most aggressive enterprise anti-bot systems. For sites defended by advanced fingerprinting and CAPTCHA walls, a premium provider like Bright Data — with its larger network and dedicated unblocking tools — remains the stronger option.
